Answers

Frequently asked.

What exactly does Keel replace?

Your project tool, team chat, CRM, invoicing/finance, lead-gen, scheduling and shared-file storage — in one login. Most agencies retire six to eight subscriptions when they move.

How does seat-based pricing work?

You pay by the number of people in your workspace, and the plan tier sets which features are unlocked. Unlimited clients and projects on every plan — you're never charged per client.

Does everyone see everything?

No. Keel has seven real roles. Super Admins and Admins see the whole business; project managers, designers and developers see only the projects they're on. You control it centrally.

Can we connect Google Workspace?

Yes — one "Connect Google" and Keel sends email as Gmail, books meetings with Meet links, and stores files in your Drive. Setup takes minutes, and files live in a dedicated space, not your personal Drive.

Is our data secure?

Encrypted secrets and shared logins, signed sessions, role-based access, and forced password resets for new members. You own your data and can export it.

How long to get started?

Usually a day. Add your team, connect Google, and import clients — we'll help you migrate from your current tools during onboarding.
